Signals Worth Watching

Key shifts that may shape the next generation of AI products, interfaces, and ecosystems.

Agents Become the New Interface

AI begins coordinating work across tools, files, browsers, and workflows.

Multimodal Becomes Default

Small Models Move Into Devices

Trust Becomes Infrastructure
