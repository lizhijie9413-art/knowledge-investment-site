    AI Trend Signals Worth Watching

    The content is split into complementary angles: model capability, application entry points, infrastructure, risk governance, business models, and human-AI relationships.

    AI Agents Become the New Interface

    AI is moving from chat windows to task execution, connecting files, browsers, databases, payments, and enterprise systems.

    Multimodal Becomes the Default

    Text, images, audio, video, and spatial data will be understood together, enabling richer visual and experiential content.

    Small Models Move Into Devices

    Phones, computers, vehicles, and robots will gain local intelligence, turning AI from a cloud service into an ambient capability.

    AI Content Needs a Trust Layer

    Watermarking, provenance, fact checking, and rights management will become basic infrastructure for content ecosystems.

    Domain Knowledge Becomes the Moat

    The most valuable AI products are not just model wrappers. They accumulate workflows, data, feedback loops, and delivery capability.

    Humans Become Orchestrators

    The key skill is no longer memorizing everything. It is asking better questions, judging outputs, and coordinating intelligent systems.

    The Future Is Not One Date, But a Set of Approaching Scenarios

    2027 to 2029

    Agent Teams Enter Everyday Workflows
